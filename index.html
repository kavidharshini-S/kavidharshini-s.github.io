Voice2Sell

Speak · Sell · Grow

Small shop owners often struggle to sell online because many e-commerce platforms are complex and need typing and digital skills. Voice2Sell lets them describe a product by speaking. The speech becomes editable name, price and description, and after the seller confirms it, the product appears in an online shop.

What could go wrong

Speech recognition may miss a word or an accent. So nothing goes live straight from the microphone: the seller edits and confirms first. Later: more regional languages, stock updates, order management.

Who it helps

  • Local shop owners, small retailers, independent sellers
  • Sellers who prefer speaking to typing
  • Customers looking for local products online
